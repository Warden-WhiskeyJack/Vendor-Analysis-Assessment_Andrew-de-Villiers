# Department Assignment Rubric

## Departments (use EXACTLY these)
Engineering, Facilities, G&A, Legal, M&A, Marketing, SaaS, Product, Professional Services, Sales, Support, Finance

## Assignment Guidelines

**Engineering**: Development tools, infrastructure, code repositories, CI/CD, cloud hosting, developer productivity

**Facilities**: Office space, physical security, utilities, building maintenance, cleaning, moving services

**G&A**: HR/People tools (payroll, benefits, recruiting), IT/Security tools (endpoint security, device management), insurance, travel, corporate events, office supplies, general admin

**Legal**: Contract management, IP services, legal research, compliance tools, e-discovery

**M&A**: Deal rooms, transaction advisory, due diligence platforms, integration consultants

**Marketing**: Advertising, content tools, social media, SEO, email campaigns, marketing automation, analytics, brand management

**SaaS**: Software subscriptions serving multiple departments (collaboration, communication, productivity suites)

**Product**: Product analytics, user research, design tools, prototyping, A/B testing, product management platforms

**Professional Services**: Consulting, accounting/audit, tax advisory, specialized expertise (non-legal)

**Sales**: CRM, sales enablement, lead generation, sales analytics, proposal software

**Support**: Customer service platforms, ticketing systems, knowledge bases, chat tools, support analytics

**Finance**: Accounting software, FP&A tools, expense management, invoicing, banking services, financial reporting

## Tie-Break Rules

1. **Function over delivery model**: Categorize by business function first (e.g., a SaaS CRM tool → Sales, not SaaS)
2. **Primary use case**: If multi-purpose, assign to the department with highest usage/value
3. **HR/People → G&A**, **IT/Security → G&A**
