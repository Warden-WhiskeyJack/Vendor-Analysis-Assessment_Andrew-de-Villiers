# EXECUTIVE MEMO: Vendor Portfolio Optimization

**TO:** CEO / CFO
**FROM:** Finance Operations
**DATE:** January 2026
**RE:** Recommended Actions to Reduce Vendor Spend by $950K–$1.3M Annually

---

## Summary

Our vendor portfolio analysis identified **$7.9M in annual spend across 386 vendors**. Three high-impact opportunities exist to reduce costs by **$950K–$1.3M per year** (12–16% savings) without operational disruption.

---

## Key Finding: CRM Spend Is 13x Industry Standard

Salesforce accounts for **$3.1M (39.5% of total vendor spend)**. Industry benchmarks indicate CRM should represent **1–3% of vendor spend**—we are at nearly 40%. This single contract represents our largest cost reduction opportunity.

---

## Top 3 Recommendations

### 1. Renegotiate Salesforce Contract — $467K–$779K/year savings

| Current Spend | Savings Range | Implementation |
|---------------|---------------|----------------|
| $3,117,226 | 15–25% reduction | Medium complexity |

**Action required:** Conduct license audit and initiate competitive RFP or renewal negotiation. We already use HubSpot ($32K/year), providing negotiation leverage.

**Timeline consideration:** Align with 12–18 month contract renewal cycle.

---

### 2. Consolidate Duplicate Vendors — ~$460K/year savings

| Overlapping Spend | Consolidation Opportunities | Implementation |
|-------------------|----------------------------|----------------|
| ~$1,100,000 | 15–20 vendor groups | High complexity |

**Highest-value consolidation targets:**
- Coworking spaces (4 overlapping vendors): $739K
- M&A Advisory (3 firms): $85K
- Consulting services (2 entities): $84K
- Recruiting/HR (6 vendors): $103K

*Note: Initial screening flagged 52 vendor groups, but detailed review shows ~30 are category groupings (e.g., multiple restaurants) rather than true duplicates. The 15–20 actionable opportunities above represent real consolidation potential.*

**Action required:** Standardize to single preferred vendor per category where regional requirements permit.

---

### 3. Terminate Low-Value Vendors — ~$87K/year savings

| Flagged Spend | Vendors | Implementation |
|---------------|---------|----------------|
| $108,652 | 57 vendors | Low complexity |

**Action required:** Conduct 90-day stakeholder review. Terminate vendors without validated business need. Target 80% of flagged spend.

---

## Investment Required

These initiatives require:
- Procurement team capacity for contract negotiations
- Cross-functional stakeholder alignment on vendor standardization
- 90-day review cycle for termination decisions

No external consulting or technology investment required.

---

## Recommendation

**Approve immediate action on all three initiatives.** The Salesforce negotiation alone could fund the entire effort within the first year while creating an additional $300K–$500K in ongoing savings.

Total projected annual benefit: **$950K–$1.3M**
